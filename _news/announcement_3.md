On October 3, 2025, I completed my **PhD in Geodetic Sciences** at the Federal University of Paraná (UFPR), with a
sandwich research period at the Politecnico di Milano (Oct 2023–Mar 2024). My dissertation, *Toward a Global
Pedestrian-Centered Map: An Open Framework for Mapping, Validating and Publishing Active Mobility Networks*,
was advised by Silvana Philippi Camboim and co-advised by Daniel Rodrigues dos Santos.

Today, August 4, 2026, I am joining the **Federal Technological University of Paraná (UTFPR)**, Pato Branco
campus, as a full-time Professor. I'm looking forward to continuing research on accessibility mapping,
collaborative cartography and open geospatial tools alongside teaching.
